# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct v2.1

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion or belief, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that are appropriate in, and conducive
to, the well-being of the community and to the specific needs of the problem
domain.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  thereby preventing harm or injury from occurring
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, or intimate or sexual attention or
  advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting
- Disruptive behavior in discussions or reviews
- Unsolicited technical criticism that is not constructive or that ignores
  established project conventions

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our
standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for
moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the
repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), documentation, CI pipelines,
and any other space where an individual represents the project or community.

Representation of a project may include, but is not limited to:
contributing code or documentation, opening issues, pull requests, code review,
and participation in community discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainer at the repository owner's preferred
contact. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter. The identity of anyone who reports a concern will not be disclosed
publicly without the reporter's consent.

We hold the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject anything that
violates this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently restrict any
individual for conduct that we determine is inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.
